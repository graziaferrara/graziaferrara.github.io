Grazia Ferrara

Hi, I am Grazia!

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the University of Salerno, co-advised by prof. Vincenzo Auletta and prof. Diodato Ferraioli and affilated with the CORE Lab.

I am enrolled in the Information Technologies for Digital Medicine curriculum and I work with AI and Multi-Agent Systems.

Previously, I completed both my B.S. and M.S. in Computer Engineering at the DIEM department of the University of Salerno.
